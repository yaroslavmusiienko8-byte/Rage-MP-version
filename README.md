# 🇺🇦 Rage MP version

**Rage MP** is a multiplayer balance mod for *Victoria 3*, developed by members of the Ukrainian strategy-gaming community Rage Kingdom.

The mod focuses on improving multiplayer balance, reducing overpowered strategies, and creating a more competitive and enjoyable MP experience.

## 🗺️ Roadmap

### 🌍 Global Changes

1. **🌸 Springtime of the Peoples**
   - Rework *Springtime of the Peoples* to remove excessive AI-driven randomness from multiplayer campaigns.

2. **🤝 Power Bloc Rework**
   - Allow a Power Bloc to change its main principle without losing its members, at the cost of mandates and Power Bloc cohesion.
   - Add new Power Bloc principles and rebalance the existing ones.
   - Reduce the leverage required to invite countries into a Power Bloc.
   - Rebalance Power Bloc cohesion.
   - Add new mandate-generation modifiers for Eastern countries, allowing them to influence global diplomacy from approximately 1865–1880 onward.

3. **🌍 Colonization Rework**
   - Allow countries with Tier I regional involvement to establish colonies, enabling more Great Powers to participate in the colonization of Africa. Encourage colonial competition and wars over African colonies.
   - Rework creatable colonial administrations and add more colony types.
   - Merge selected African cultures to improve game performance and reduce late-game lag.
   - Increase colony growth speed through technological and institutional advancements.

4. **⚖️ New Laws**
   - **Ecology Laws:** Introduce a dedicated law group for pollution management and remove pollution reduction from healthcare laws.
   - **Taxation Laws:** Split the current taxation system into several laws, allowing more precise control over a country's fiscal model.
   - **Economic Laws:** Split economic-system mechanics across multiple law groups to provide deeper and more flexible economic customization.

### 🏳️ Country Changes

1. **🇫🇷 France**
   - Reduce the penalties from **The Divided Monarchists** to make France more stable.
   - Fix and clarify the path to the **Second Republic** through a republican revolt so prevent France from gaining an excessive number of beneficial laws through the revolution and triger SP for other from start of the game.
   - Prevent France from triggering **Springtime of the Peoples** across Europe too early.

2. **🇯🇵 Japan**
   - Adjust the ages and birth dates of emperors and shoguns to slightly accelerate the Meiji Restoration and Japan's modernization.
   - Allow Japan to complete its internal reforms earlier and begin participating in global politics sooner.
